01 / about

About Me

I got into programming because I wanted to understand how software actually works — not just use it. That curiosity turned into a habit: learn a concept, then build something small with it until it finally makes sense.

Today most of my work is in Java and Python, with a growing focus on backend development — REST APIs, databases, and the engineering decisions that make an application reliable. The part I enjoy most is problem-solving: taking a messy requirement and breaking it down into something that can actually be built and maintained.

Outside coursework, I build personal projects like LegalMate AI and AttendLock — each one taught me more than any tutorial could. My next step is a software engineering internship where I can contribute to real production systems.
